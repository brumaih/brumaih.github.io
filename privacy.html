Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Session Transcript Pipeline is a personal desktop application under development. This policy describes the intended handling of data by its Google Drive integration. It is used by its owner to preserve and review their own conversations.

Data the application uses

The application processes local Claude Code and Codex session records, rendered transcript text, and archive metadata such as session identifiers, titles, timestamps, file identifiers, and checksums. When authorized, it accesses the Google Drive files it creates or that the owner explicitly authorizes for the app. It stores the OAuth authorization locally so the owner can use the integration again.

Purpose and storage

This data is used to create, update, organize, retrieve, and search the owner's transcript archive. The archive stores raw records and readable copies in the owner's Google Drive, with local processing and caching. Google Drive is the sole archive destination selected by the owner. The public information website does not receive or display transcript contents or OAuth credentials.

Sharing and use

Archive contents are sent to Google Drive at the owner's direction. The owner may separately authorize an assistant service to read their archive for discussion or analysis; that service has its own connection and policies. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.

Session Transcript Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

The archive is designed for long-term preservation without automatic expiry. Files remain until the owner deliberately removes them. The owner can revoke the application's access in their Google Account connections; revocation stops future access and does not itself delete files already stored in Drive.

This website

These are static information pages hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no application sign-in, forms, analytics scripts, or advertising. GitHub processes hosting request information, including visitor IP addresses, as described in its Privacy Statement.

Contact and updates

The application is maintained by brumaih. The support address also appears on the application's Google authorization screen. This policy will be updated when the application's data handling changes.
