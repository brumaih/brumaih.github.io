Terms of use

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Personal use

Session Transcript Pipeline is being developed for its owner's personal use. This website explains the application and does not offer public accounts, subscriptions, or transcript hosting for other users.

Google Drive authorization

The integration operates only after the account owner grants access through Google. Use is limited to records and files the owner is authorized to process. Google services remain subject to their applicable terms and account storage limits.

Development status

The application is under development. Features described on this site represent its planned behavior. Archive migration and independent backup validation must be completed before relying on the new pipeline for preservation.

Ownership and control

The owner retains control of their transcript archive. Publishing these information pages does not publish the archive. Access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account, as explained in the privacy policy.

Maintenance

The application and these pages may be updated as development proceeds. Project contact is available through the maintainer's GitHub profile and the support address shown on the Google authorization screen.
